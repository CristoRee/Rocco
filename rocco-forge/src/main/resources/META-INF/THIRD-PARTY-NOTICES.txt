Rocco includes the following third-party software, used to play music from links.
Each one keeps its own license. Rocco does not modify them, except that the lavaplayer
sources that extract audio from websites are left out of the jar. Full license texts are
in META-INF/licenses (Apache-2.0, LGPL-2.1 and the Fraunhofer FDK AAC license).

lavaplayer 2.2.7 (dev.arbjerg:lavaplayer, lava-common, lavaplayer-natives)
  https://github.com/lavalink-devs/lavaplayer
  Apache License 2.0 - https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
  The native libraries (natives/*) bundle third-party decoders:
    libmpg123 - GNU LGPL 2.1 (https://www.mpg123.de). It is loaded as a separate dynamic
      library (natives/win-x86-64/libmpg123-0.dll and inside libconnector) and can be replaced.
    Opus (BSD), libvorbis/libogg (BSD), libsamplerate (BSD), fdk-aac (Fraunhofer FDK AAC license).

Jackson 2.15.2 (com.fasterxml.jackson.core) - Apache License 2.0
  https://github.com/FasterXML/jackson
jsoup 1.16.1 (org.jsoup) - MIT License
  https://jsoup.org
JSON-java 20240303 (org.json) - Public Domain
  https://github.com/stleary/JSON-java
Base64 2.3.9 (net.iharder) - Public Domain

These libraries are packaged with their packages renamed under com.cristoree.rocco.lib
(except lavaplayer), so they do not clash with other mods.
