# Rocco: your AI adventure companion

**Rocco sprouts from the ground next to you and goes with you everywhere.** He chats with his own personality (cocky, but lovable), follows you flying, swimming and by boat, works the farm, crafts, cooks, enchants your tools, drinks mate, fights by your side with his own armor and weapons, and, if you want, talks out loud and listens through your microphone.

He works **without any account or key**: he comes with hundreds of built-in lines, understands orders in Spanish and comments on what happens. A free AI key makes him chat for real.

> **Language:** Rocco speaks **Spanish**. The menus, his built-in lines and the chat orders without AI are in Spanish. With an AI key he can reply in English: in his menu (sneak + right-click) open **Personalidad** and set **Dialect** to **Automatic**, and he follows your game language.

---

## ✨ What he does

- **Real conversations:** with his own personality, he remembers what you told him and brings it up later. Three modes: Chill, No filter and Custom.
- **Follows you everywhere:** flies with you when you use an elytra, floats on his duck ring and dives with goggles, and paddles alongside you in his own little pumpkin boat. He never gets lost.
- **Looks after your home while you explore:** harvests, shears, breeds animals (without overdoing it), collects eggs and wool and stores everything. When you come back he tells you what he did.
- **Works:** farms and harvests, crafts with the real recipes (and tells you what's missing), cooks, chops trees and replants them, lights up areas, sorts and stores things in your chests, fishes, breeds animals and trades with villagers.
- **Enchants your gear** once a day, like an enchanting table.
- **Mate:** he drinks mate and shares one with you if you ask.
- **Truco:** "¿jugamos un truco?" and he brings out a table and a chair for everyone (up to 7 players). Uruguayan with muestra or Argentine, flor, envido and all the calls, mate between hands, and his own way of playing.
- **Asado and tortas fritas:** he grills the raw meat you give him on his little grill and fries tortas fritas while sipping mate. Right-click the grill or the bowl to take one.
- **3D armor and weapons:** each material with its own style (a little gold crown, a diamond unicorn horn, netherite cat ears...), plus swords, axes, trident, bow, crossbow and shield with their own models.
- **Fights by your side:** defends you, attacks what you attack and fights when you ask him ("atacá a ese zombie"). You choose who he can attack.
- **If he dies, he comes back:** after 15 seconds he reappears buried near you. With an ender chest he gains the power to never lose his things.
- **Eats and heals:** heals over time, faster after eating, and if you allow it he drinks potions and eats golden apples in a pinch.
- **Plays music:** give him a link to an audio file or an internet radio and it plays from him while he sings and dances to the beat. And if a jukebox plays nearby, he dances.
- **Backpacks:** 6 backpacks crafted in order that grow his inventory up to 216 slots. The first starts with a gift from him when you become friends; the last one, with another gift that only comes after the great adventures. You can also open them with right-click in your hand, and choose how they look on Rocco.
- **On horseback:** he rides in front of you. And on your wolf, if you let him.
- **Roco Cola:** his favorite soda (and don't call it Coca Cola). He shares one with you, and what comes after pushes mobs away. The empty bottle can be thrown or broken into a broken bottle, and sometimes he fights with one.
- **Gifts:** if you leave for a while, when you come back he has left you things he collected. And every now and then he gives you something rare.
- **Voice and microphone (optional):** talks out loud with Fish Audio, imitates character voices and listens with Deepgram. Without a Fish Audio key he talks in text. On a server, the key and the voice are those of whoever hosts it.

## 🌱 Getting started

1. Install Forge (1.20.1) or NeoForge (1.21.1, 26.3), GeckoLib and Rocco.
2. Use Rocco's spawn egg (spawn eggs tab) and right-click the little plant.
3. Talk to him in chat. **Sneak + right-click** opens his menu.

To make him chat with AI, add a free key (Groq, Gemini or OpenRouter, or Ollama on your PC) in **Mods > Rocco > Config > IA (cerebro)**.

## 🔒 Privacy and external services

Without keys, Rocco **connects to nothing** (except the link of a song you give him, see below). Only if you add a key does he connect to that service:

- **AI** (the provider you choose): what you say to him, the recent chat and a summary of what is happening in the game.
- **Fish Audio**: the text he is going to say out loud, or the name of a character to look up their public voice.
- **Deepgram**: your microphone audio, only while you hold the key (or with voice detection).
- **The site of a song link**: when you or another player nearby gives him a link, your game opens it to play it, like a browser. A link from another player that points to your own computer or local network is never opened. You can turn music off in **Config > Vida y rutina > Música**.

Keys stay only in your config. The mod collects no data and sends nothing anywhere else. It contains no voices of real people.
